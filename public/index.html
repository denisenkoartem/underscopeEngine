Vehicle

17 characters, on the dash or the driver's door jamb.
Tell us, in your own words:
  • the title: clean, salvage, rebuilt, flood, non-repairable or lemon; whether you have it in hand; any open lien
  • the mileage and whether it is actual, the state it is in, and who is selling (dealer, insurer, fleet, lender or private owner)
  • whether it runs and drives, whether the keys are with it, and how the engine and transmission are
  • where it was hit, what is damaged part by part, and whether the rest is fine
  • whether the airbags deployed, the frame is damaged, anything is leaking, any flood or fire signs, and whether the catalytic converter is there
  • the clean wholesale value (same year, model and mileage, no damage) and what at least 3 similar damaged vehicles sold for
  • a repair quote if you have one, the lowest price you would accept, and any deadline or storage fees
Add anything else you know about it: warning lights, fault codes, noises, what was already repaired.
